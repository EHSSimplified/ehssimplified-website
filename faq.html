Safety Consulting for Small Businesses.
Straight Answers, No Sales Pitch.

The questions business leaders ask us most, before they ever book a call.

What does a fractional EHS consultant actually do for a small business?

A fractional EHS consultant gives your business ongoing access to a Certified Safety Professional (CSP) without the cost of a full-time hire. With EHS Simplified, Agentic EHS handles the day-to-day load (written programs, training, inspections, SDS library and incident reporting), a CSP reviews the work, and you get CSP time every month for the judgment calls. Ongoing support, not a one-time visit.

We haven't had an OSHA violation — do we actually need a safety consultant?

A clean inspection history usually means you haven't been checked yet, not that you're fully compliant. Most small businesses have real gaps — missing written programs, outdated training records, undocumented hazard assessments — that only surface during an audit, an insurance renewal, or after an incident. Our job is to find and close those gaps before they cost you.

What's the difference between hiring a full-time EHS manager and using a fractional program?

A full-time hire gives you one person's time; a fractional program gives you a system plus expert oversight. A single EHS manager at a company under 100 employees is often stretched thin, and if they leave, your program leaves with them. A fractional program pairs your team with a CSP, a documented program, and Agentic EHS, which keeps inspections, training, and records running even as staff changes.

How quickly can you get us OSHA-compliant?

Most clients see their highest-risk gaps addressed within the first 30-60 days. We start with a safety assessment to identify what's missing or out of date — written programs, training records, hazard communication, machine guarding, lockout/tagout — then prioritize fixes by risk level and inspection likelihood. Full program maturity typically builds over two to three quarters.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business where people do real work, and that's every business: manufacturing and machine shops, construction and trades, warehousing, auto and equipment repair, food and beverage, labs, and offices too. The common thread is real hazards (machines, chemicals, vehicles, lifting, even desks and screens) and no in-house safety department to manage them.

Can a safety consultant actually lower our workers' comp costs?

Fewer recordable injuries is the most direct way to reduce what you pay for workers' comp over time. A managed safety program reduces incident frequency through better training, hazard controls, and faster near-miss reporting — which is what ultimately drives your premiums down over time.

What happens during an OSHA inspection, and can you help us prepare?

Yes — inspection readiness is one of the most common reasons companies call us. An OSHA compliance officer will typically review your written programs and injury logs, walk your workplace, and interview employees. We help you prepare documentation in advance, review your program against what an inspector looks for, and advise you in real time if an inspection happens.

Do you provide safety training for our production employees, not just paperwork?

Yes — training is built into every plan, not sold as a separate add-on. That includes new-hire safety onboarding, OSHA-required topics (hazard communication, lockout/tagout, PPE, powered industrial trucks), delivered as ready-to-use training materials your supervisors run with your team.

How is this different from an enterprise EHS software platform?

Enterprise EHS platforms are typically built and priced for large, multi-site organizations with internal safety staff to run them. Agentic EHS does the first draft for you and comes with a real CSP who reviews the work and answers your questions, so you're not left to configure and run a complex system yourself with no safety background on staff.

Does our safety consultant need to be a Certified Safety Professional (CSP)?

The CSP credential matters because it verifies real, tested expertise across OSHA regulations, hazard recognition, and safety management — not just familiarity with paperwork. For decisions about machine guarding, chemical exposure, or incident investigation, that depth of knowledge is what keeps advice defensible if OSHA or your insurer ever questions it.

How does a fractional EHS program actually work month to month?

Agentic EHS does the day-to-day work: it drafts your written programs and training, plans each day's safety tasks, runs inspections and reporting from a phone, and keeps the compliance calendar. Nothing it drafts is final until a named person approves it. Every plan includes Certified Safety Professional time each month, and a time meter shows what's been used so there are no surprise bills.
