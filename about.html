Founder & Consultant, EHS Simplified

With over 25 years of hands-on Environmental Health & Safety experience, Aaron Leff built EHS Simplified because he saw the same gap everywhere: businesses carrying full OSHA compliance obligations without access to the caliber of safety expertise that large enterprises take for granted.

EHS Simplified operates like an outsourced EHS department — a Certified Safety Professional's expertise delivered on a fractional basis, backed by a mobile safety platform your team can actually use on the job site, in the shop, or on the floor. It's the same level of program management you'd expect from an in-house safety department, built to scale up or down as your business needs it.

The CSP expertise behind it means the platform isn't just functional — it's built right. The inspection checklists, training content, and hazard controls reflect 25 years of real-world EHS work across industries, and that expertise is available directly, every month, on every tier.
