Every plan includes

  • Written safety programs drafted for your site, approved by a named person
  • Employee training with pictures, quizzes, sign-offs and records
  • “My day”: your safety work laid out each morning, desk work and floor work
  • Inspections, hazard and close-call reporting, and actions to close the loop
  • Ergonomic assessments from a phone photo, with fixes ranked best first
  • Compliance calendar, SDS library, risk assessments and injury logs
  • Escalate to a CSP from any screen, with a time meter so there are no surprise bills
  • Workers free and unlimited; anyone can ask a safety question
