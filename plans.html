Common Questions

Before You Commit

Do plans come with a set number of hours?
No. You're buying a service, not billable hours. Each plan is defined by what you get — the check-ins, programs, and deliverables listed — plus reasonable day-to-day guidance from your EHS Manager. If your needs consistently outgrow your plan, we'll say so honestly and talk through the right fit.
Is this a remote service?
Mostly, yes. Gap assessments, written programs, training materials, recordkeeping, and guidance are all delivered remotely by phone, video, and the app. Safety Executive adds a quarterly site visit, and on-site work is available on any plan by quote.
How does the gap assessment work?
Starting at Safety Partnered, we open with a one-time compliance gap assessment of your programs, records, and operations against the OSHA standards that apply to you. You get written findings and a prioritized corrective action plan. From there, regular check-ins track that plan to completion.
Do you perform mock OSHA audits or represent us during an inspection?
No. We don't run mock OSHA audits or act as your representative with OSHA. What we do is a compliance gap assessment that shows where you stand, then guide you through inspection readiness and citation response so you know exactly what to do and say.
What exactly is a "custom safety policy"?
A written, OSHA-aligned policy document built specifically for your business — your industry, your hazards, your language. Not a generic template with your name pasted in. Examples include a Hazard Communication Program, Lockout/Tagout Policy, Fall Protection Plan, or Respiratory Protection Program.
What is a toolbox talk?
A short, focused safety topic your supervisor can run in 10–15 minutes before a shift — written for your crew, in plain language, on a hazard relevant to your work right now. Delivered as a ready-to-use document each month.
What does OSHA 300 log management include?
We track, maintain, and file your OSHA injury and illness records — the 300 Log, 300A Annual Summary, and 301 Incident Reports — so you're always compliant and ready if OSHA comes knocking.
How does the "step down" process work?
As your safety program matures, we'll have an honest conversation about whether you still need the same level of support. If your culture is strong and your team is running safety confidently, we'll recommend moving to a lower tier — and we mean it. A Safety Executive client who steps down to Safety Guided after 18 months is a success story, not a lost account.
What happens if we have an incident on the Safety Standby or Guided plan?
Additional consulting is available on every plan at a discount off our standard rate. On Safety Embedded and above, incident investigation guidance is included in your plan.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees on any plan. Done-for-you app configuration is included from day one — we load your SDS library, emergency contacts, and document portal as part of onboarding.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Absolutely. If your business grows or you take on new work with higher hazard exposure, upgrading is simple. Upgrades take effect immediately; we'll prorate the difference.
