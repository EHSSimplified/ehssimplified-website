The Problem

More Safety Work Than Hours in the Day.
We Fix the Math.

Whether safety landed on top of your real job or it is your whole job, the paperwork eats the hours you should be spending on the floor with your people.

01

The hours add up fast

Writing one safety program from scratch can take days. Add training, inspections, records and chasing fixes, and it's easily a day a week or more, time worth far more than the software that could do most of it.

02

Most EHS software adds work

Big EHS platforms give you more screens to fill in and someone to administer them. Whether safety is your whole job or part of it, you need a tool that does the first draft, so you can do the expert part.

03

Doing nothing isn't free

A serious OSHA violation can cost up to $16,550. One lost-time injury can raise workers' comp costs for years. Getting the basics in place helps reduce that exposure.

Someone at $60 an hour spending 8 hours a week on safety puts about $2,080 a month of their time into it. Agentic EHS starts at $449. Work out your own numbers →
