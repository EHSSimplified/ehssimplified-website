The Problem

The Safety Programs Large Companies Run.
Built for Businesses of Any Size.

Documented policies, audits, and dedicated oversight — the hallmarks of a mature safety program — have historically required an in-house department or a national consulting retainer. Not anymore.

01

National platforms price out growing businesses

Platforms like VelocityEHS and Benchmark Gensuite can run into the thousands per month and typically require a dedicated administrator to run them. Built for companies with in-house safety staff, not the businesses that need protection most.

02

Spreadsheets and binders don't scale

Paper programs get ignored, and shared drives get outgrown fast. A safety program your team can't access in the field, or that can't grow with the business, isn't really a safety program.

03

Doing nothing costs more

OSHA serious violation fines run up to $16,550 per violation. A single lost-time injury can drive workers' comp premiums up for years. The cost of inaction is not zero.

Starting at $129/month, your business gets a dedicated fractional EHS Manager and a mobile safety platform built by a CSP — inspections, training, hazard reporting, and SDS access, backed by the same expertise a full-time safety department would provide.
