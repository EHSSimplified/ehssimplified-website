Today I'm launching Agentic EHS, the new software from EHS Simplified. It drafts your written safety programs, builds your training, plans your inspections and lays out your safety work each morning. A Certified Safety Professional checks the work and is a tap away when you need one. It replaces the EHS Simplified mobile app.

## Why did I build Agentic EHS?

Because the people I work with don't have a time problem they can hire their way out of.

In most small plants, safety lands on someone who already has a full-time job: the plant manager, the operations lead, the HR manager or the owner. Writing one safety program from scratch can take days. Add training, inspections, records and chasing fixes, and it eats the hours you should spend on the floor with your people.

Most EHS software doesn't fix that. It gives you more screens to fill in. I wanted a tool that does the first draft, so you can do the part only you can do.

## What does Agentic EHS do?

Every plan includes:

- **Written safety programs** — drafted for your site, final only when a named person approves them
- **Training** — with pictures, quizzes, sign-offs and records
- **"My day"** — your safety work laid out each morning, desk work and floor work
- **Inspections and reporting** — hazards, close calls and the actions that close them out
- **Ergonomic assessments** — from a phone photo, with fixes ranked best first
- **Compliance calendar** — plus your SDS library, risk assessments and injury logs
- **CSP time every month** — escalate from any screen, with a time meter so there are no surprise bills

Workers are always free, and anyone can ask a safety question.

## How does it work?

- **Step 1: Tell it about your site.** Answer a few plain-English questions about your work, equipment and chemicals. No safety jargon needed.
- **Step 2: It drafts, you approve.** Programs, training, checklists and your daily plan are drafted for your site. A CSP reviews the work.
- **Step 3: Your team runs it from a phone.** Supervisors and employees do inspections, report hazards and take training on the floor.

## Is AI safe to use for a safety program?

Only with a person in charge of it. That's the rule I built this around. The AI writes the first draft. Nothing is final until a named person at your company approves it, and a CSP is there to check the work and answer the hard questions. In 25 years of EHS work, I've learned the expert part is judgment, not typing. Agentic EHS takes the typing.
