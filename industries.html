Who We Serve

Built for Small Manufacturers

EHS Simplified is a CSP-led consulting practice built for manufacturers under 100 employees — metal fabrication, machining, assembly, and food & beverage production. We focus where the real production-floor hazards are: machinery, chemicals, powered industrial trucks, and ergonomics.

⚙️

Manufacturing & Production

Lockout/tagout programs, machine guarding, hazard communication, powered industrial truck safety, and PPE assessments — built for metal fabrication, machining, and assembly operations under 100 employees.

🥫

Food & Beverage Manufacturing

Sanitation-compatible safety programs, chemical handling, ergonomics, and equipment guarding for food and beverage production lines.
